Onboarding Lifecycle

Step 01

Chubb’s partnership team onboards the partner on Chubb’s digital platform – Chubb Studio

Step 02

Products are configured for the partner on Chubb Studio Canvas

Step 03

Partner receives an invitation to view their products & configuration on Chubb Studio Connect

Step 04

Partner teams leverage Chubb Studio Connect self-servicing capabilities to onboard additional team members, onboard their application, view & access products configured for them, view/access/test out APIs, view/access other stats & insights
