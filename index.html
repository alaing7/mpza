  • The Department of Informatics plays a leading role in shaping the digital age. We deal with data protection, data science, entrepreneurial and social innovation, and sustainable development.

  • Those who study Informatics with us learn to help shape the digital world of tomorrow – whether in online retail, healthcare, finance or the media.

  • We offer five major specializations on the Master’s level: Information Systems, Software Systems, Human-Centered Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science. We also offer attractive minor specializations.
